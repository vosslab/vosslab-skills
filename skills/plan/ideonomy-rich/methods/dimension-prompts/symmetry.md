# Dimension prompt: symmetry

Is the idea symmetric, asymmetric, or anti-symmetric?

Possible values: **fully symmetric / mostly symmetric / asymmetric / anti-symmetric.**

Substitution test: take an asymmetric version (e.g., a relationship where one party gives and the other receives) and make it symmetric (both give, both receive). Or break a symmetry deliberately. Symmetry-breaking is one of the most generative ideational moves — most novel structures arise this way.
