# Dimension prompt: rate

How fast does this idea operate? What's its characteristic timescale?

Possible values: **instantaneous / seconds / minutes / hours / days / years / geological.**

Substitution test: speed it up by 1000×. Slow it down by 1000×. What is it now? Many ideas have a "natural" rate that isn't actually load-bearing — varying it surfaces a population of related ideas (instant version, slow-motion version) that may be the right answer for a different context.
