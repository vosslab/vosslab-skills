# Dimension prompt: autonomy

Is this idea self-driven, externally driven, or jointly driven?

Possible values: **self-driven / mostly-autonomous / co-driven / mostly-driven / fully-controlled.**

Substitution test: take a self-driven version (something that runs on its own) and make it externally controlled. Take an externally controlled version and make it autonomous. The autonomy-axis is doing more work in most ideas than people notice — it's where the politics usually live.
