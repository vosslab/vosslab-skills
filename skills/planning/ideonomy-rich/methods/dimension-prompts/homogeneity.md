# Dimension prompt: homogeneity

Is the idea internally all-the-same, or differentiated?

Possible values: **homogenous / mostly-homogenous / mixed / mostly-differentiated / fully-differentiated.**

Substitution test: hold every other property constant; swap homogenous → heterogenous. A homogenous-tree (every leaf identical) vs a heterogenous-tree (every leaf different). Both exist; both are useful in different contexts.

Grace lists Homogeneity as one of the five exemplary dimensions in her properties-and-dimensions essay.
