# Dimension prompt: modularity

Are the parts of this idea tightly coupled, or modular and recombinable?

Possible values: **monolithic / loosely-coupled / modular / fully-modular.**

Substitution test: take a monolithic version of the idea and break it into modules — what are the modules, and what are their interfaces? Take a modular version and fuse it — what's the monolith? Modularity-substitution reveals where the seams want to be.
