# Dimension prompt: polarity

Is the idea valued positively, negatively, ambivalently, or value-neutrally?

Possible values: **strongly positive / mildly positive / ambivalent / mildly negative / strongly negative / context-dependent.**

Substitution test: take a positively-valued idea and re-examine it as if it were negative (the dark version of community, the dark version of sharing). Take a negatively-valued idea and find its positive analogue (Gunkel's *proseases* are the positive analogue of *diseases*; Grace cites this as a canonical ideonomic move). Polarity-flips are reliably generative.
