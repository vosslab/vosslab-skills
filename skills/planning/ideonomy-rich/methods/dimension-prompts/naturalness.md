# Dimension prompt: naturalness

Is this idea natural or man-made?

Possible values: **fully natural / partially man-made / hybrid / mostly man-made / fully synthetic.**

Substitution test: hold everything else constant; swap natural → man-made. Grace's example: a natural tree {Natural, Heterogenous, Non-hierarchical, Variably-lived, Complex} substituted to {Man-made, Heterogenous, Non-hierarchical, Variably-lived, Complex} produces synthetic forests, AI-generated tree art, sculpted topiary forests — all coherent variants.

Grace lists Naturalness as the first of her five exemplary dimensions.
