# Dimension prompt: complexity

Is the idea simple or complex?

Possible values: **trivial / simple / moderately complex / complex / hyper-complex.**

Substitution test: produce the simplest possible version of the idea, then the most complex possible version. Both extremes are useful: the simplest version often reveals the load-bearing kernel; the most complex often exists in some niche where it's worth the cost.

Grace lists Complexity as one of the five exemplary dimensions.
