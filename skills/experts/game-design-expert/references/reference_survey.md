## How to use coverage

Use the topic's decision first, then consult its supporting passage when needed.
`strong` means direct discussion, `partial` means a useful but incomplete lens,
`thin` means limited support, and `not covered` means the source does not supply
the required guidance. A missing book is not a blocker: use the committed
references, the target's evidence, and appropriate first-principles evaluation.

## Coverage limits and ownership

The books support mastery, interest, and iteration, but prescribe no universal
surprise interval. The practical "expect the unexpected" direction comes from
the user and Steroid Quest evidence in [case_studies.md](case_studies.md).

Autonomous agent completion, efficient artwork comparisons, current TypeScript
tooling, and browser-specific APIs are **not covered** by this survey. Use
[testing_and_oracles.md](testing_and_oracles.md) for the user's completion policy,
[art_direction.md](art_direction.md) for the M.U.L.E. caution, and target platform
documentation and tests for current engineering behavior.

Human enjoyment and learning outcomes are **not established** by source inspection,
automated game completion, or agent design judgment. Use those methods for their
actual evidence and preserve the distinction in reports.
