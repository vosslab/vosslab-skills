# Local book sources

The optional research corpus is under `$HOME/MARKDOWN_BOOKS/`. Locate files by basename with
file-search tools; category directories may change. Books remain local and are not bundled.
Prefer `3d_printing_and_cad/` for the dedicated subject collection. The reviewed cookbook,
design guide, and failure reference have byte-identical copies in `technology/`; treat these
as duplicate locations, not independent evidence.
The skill works without this corpus: operational guidance and public documentation links are
included in [reference_survey.md](reference_survey.md) and [topic_index.md](topic_index.md).

## OpenSCAD and generation books

The following files are under `3d_printing_and_cad/`. Each has inspected passages in
[reference_survey.md](reference_survey.md), not a claim of full-book reading or executed examples.

- `Programming_with_OpenSCAD_A_Beginners_Guide_to_Coding_3D-Printable_Objects-2021.md`:
  incremental assembly development, module interfaces, print layouts, and calibration.
- `Simplifying_3D_Printing_with_OpenSCAD-2022.md`:
  public part modules, content-dependent geometry, sliders, and measurement gauges.
- `Creating_3D_Models_for_3D_Printing_Using_OpenSCAD-2020.md`:
  safe debug isolation, section cuts, resized mechanisms, and enclosed cavity roofs.
- `OpenSCAD_for_3D_Printing-2014.md`:
  libraries and child-geometry wrappers; historical syntax requires current verification.
- `OpenSCAD_Basics_Tutorial-2020.md`:
  arrays, repeated hull constructions, mirrored parts, and shell-thickness pitfalls.
- `Python_for_3D_Printing-2019.md`:
  external generators, wrapper semantics, and sampled surfaces; the appendix is not a vetted library.

- `OpenSCAD_Cookbook_Recipes_for_Learning_3D_Modeling_Craig-2019.md`:
  John Clark Craig's worked recipes for parameterization, functions/modules, and extrusion.

## Print design and diagnostics

- `3d_printing_and_cad/3D_Printing_For_Dummies-2023.md`:
  service conditions, conflicting load paths, mesh checks, toolpaths, and split parts.
- `3d_printing_and_cad/A_Practical_Guide_to_Design_for_Additive_Manufacturing-2020.md`:
  polymer part design, anisotropy, ribs, support access, and material-extrusion feature guidance.
- `3d_printing_and_cad/3D_Printing_Failures_Diagnose_and_Repair-2022.md`:
  fit-failure diagnosis and measured calibration; distinguish anecdotal settings from requirements.

## Background and selective applications

- `3d_printing_and_cad/3D_Printer_User_Guide_Fundamentals_Maintenance-2020.md`:
  geometry/slicer/machine boundaries; broad numerical prescriptions are not coding rules.
- `3d_printing_and_cad/Changing_Lives_Through_3-D_Printing-2021.md`:
  applications and replica-sharing context, not technical or clinical validation.
- `3d_printing_and_cad/Learning_Mathematics_in_the_Context_of_3D_Printing-2022.md`:
  dependency-driven geometry, tessellation models, and symmetric connectors; verify damaged math.
- `3d_printing_and_cad/Smart_Revolution_How_AI_and_3D_Printing_are_Transforming_Industry-2024.md`:
  requirements and manufacturing limitations, not executable optimization or proof of safety.

## Supporting engineering references

- `svg-creation/Technical_Drawing_with_Engineering_Graphics_Sixteenth_Edition-2023.md`:
  manufacturing terminology, fits, STL tessellation, and process-dependent fabrication.
- `computational_geometry/Handbook_of_Discrete_and_Computational_Geometry_Third_Edition-2018.md`:
  boundary representations, manifold topology, and triangle meshes.

Search the headings recorded in [reference_survey.md](reference_survey.md), then read the
surrounding passage. Line numbers are lookup aids for the inspected conversion, not stable IDs.
Use the current OpenSCAD manual for language semantics and version-sensitive behavior; the
books supply worked examples rather than a current API compatibility reference.
Read the original page when OCR or a formula is ambiguous. Keep copied book text out of the skill;
store concise paraphrases and source locators instead.
