# Dimension prompt: reversibility

Is this idea reversible — once it happens, can it be undone?

Possible values: **fully reversible / mostly reversible / partially / mostly irreversible / fully irreversible.**

Substitution test: take an irreversible idea (death, marriage, certain contracts) and ask what its reversible form would be. Take a reversible idea and ask what locking it in would mean. Reversibility is doing more work in most ideas than the surface vocabulary admits — and substitution often reveals an interesting variant.
