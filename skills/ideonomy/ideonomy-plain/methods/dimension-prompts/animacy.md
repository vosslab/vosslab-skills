# Dimension prompt: animacy

Is the idea, or its instances, alive, mechanical, or abstract?

Possible values: **alive / quasi-alive / mechanical / informational / abstract.**

Substitution test: an alive version of a mechanical idea, or vice versa. *Alive money* (currency that grows or dies). *Mechanical love* (ritual repetition). The substitution often produces a coined-but-coherent variant.

Grace's example of negating "the rulers are people" produced *automatocracy, zoocracy, phytocracy* — animacy-substitutions of democracy.
