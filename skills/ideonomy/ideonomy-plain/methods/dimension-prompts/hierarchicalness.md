# Dimension prompt: hierarchicalness

Does this idea involve a hierarchy, or is it flat / non-hierarchical?

Possible values: **hierarchical / partial-hierarchy / mixed / flat / counter-hierarchical.**

Substitution test: replace a hierarchical version with a flat version (or vice versa). Many institutions and structures have one form by convention while their flat-or-hierarchical alter ego exists in some other domain. Hierarchicalness-substitution surfaces those alter egos.

Grace lists Hierarchicalness as one of the five exemplary dimensions.
