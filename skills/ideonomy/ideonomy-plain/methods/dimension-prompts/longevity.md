# Dimension prompt: longevity

How long does this idea, or its instances, last?

Possible values: **shorter than a human life / about a human life / longer than a human life / variable / unbounded.**

Substitution test: hold everything else constant; swap the longevity. A 10-second version of this idea? A 10,000-year version? Each variant is a candidate.

Grace lists Longevity as one of the five exemplary dimensions in her properties-and-dimensions essay. (See `methods/operators/dimension-identification.md`.)
