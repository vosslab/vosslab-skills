# Dimension prompt: intentionality

Is this idea intentional, accidental, or emergent?

Possible values: **deliberately designed / partially designed / co-emergent / fully accidental / emergent from many small intentions.**

Substitution test: take a designed-on-purpose idea and ask what its accidental version would look like. Take an accident and ask what its designed version is. The substitution often reveals which properties of the idea were designed in vs which are artifacts of how it happens to have arisen.
