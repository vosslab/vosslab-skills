# Dimension prompt: materiality

Is the idea physical, abstract, or somewhere between?

Possible values: **fully physical / embodied-but-abstract / informational / fully abstract.**

Substitution test: an abstract idea given a physical embodiment, or a physical idea reduced to its information content. *Money* substituted across this axis: physical coin / paper / digital ledger / pure social agreement / cryptographic token. Each substitution produces a different ideational variant, and the *original* term ends up looking like one position on a longer scale.
