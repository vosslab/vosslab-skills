# Dimension prompt: visibility

Is this idea visible, hidden, latent, or invisible?

Possible values: **public / semi-visible / latent / hidden / invisible.**

Substitution test: take a public idea and make it private; take a hidden idea and surface it. Many ideas have a "shadow" version that exists in the same space but in the opposite visibility regime — public and private banking, open-source and proprietary software, public and back-channel diplomacy.
