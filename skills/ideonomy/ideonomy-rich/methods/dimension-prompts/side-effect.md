# Dimension prompt: side-effect

What does this idea produce *besides* its intended effect?

Possible values for *kind* of side effect: **none / benign / opposite-of-intent / hidden / delayed / scale-dependent.**

Substitution test: take the side-effect and treat it as the main effect. The "side effect" of social media is community formation; the "main effect" is content delivery. Swapping them yields a different idea — a community-first product where content is incidental — which is sometimes what was actually being asked for.
