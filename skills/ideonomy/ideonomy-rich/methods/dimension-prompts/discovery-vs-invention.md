# Dimension prompt: discovery-vs-invention

Was this idea discovered (it was always there, awaiting recognition) or invented (it was constructed by minds)?

Possible values: **fully discovered / mostly discovered / contested / mostly invented / fully invented.**

Substitution test: treat a "discovered" idea (e.g., a mathematical truth) as an invention. Treat an "invented" idea (e.g., a legal system) as a discovery. The substitution forces you to articulate what you mean by each — and surfaces ideas where the reframe genuinely fits.
