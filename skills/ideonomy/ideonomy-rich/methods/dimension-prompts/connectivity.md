# Dimension prompt: connectivity

How densely connected are the components of this idea? How densely is the idea connected to its surroundings?

Possible values: **isolated / sparsely connected / densely connected / fully connected.**

Substitution test: increase the connectivity to "everything connected to everything." Reduce it to "every connection severed." The two extremes are usually each their own pattern — saturation vs isolation — and the original sits somewhere in between.
